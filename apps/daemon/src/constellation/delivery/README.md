# Lead and worker delivery

`ConstellationDelivery` is scoped and starts explicitly after Session recovery and MCP revocation replay. It subscribes to committed graph and Session facts, reopens a dropped bounded subscription from its durable sequence, and derives pending work from the fold. No timer runs without a wake-worthy pending notification. Claims, settlements, proposals and answers coalesce for 20 seconds; blocking Lead questions shorten the window to 5 seconds. A busy, paused, blocked or terminal Lead keeps the same IDs queued. Recovery starts a fresh coalescing window.

A digest commits `LeadNotified` and the Session machine's `TurnStarted` together, or, when the Lead is Working on a Harness that can steer (and any Gate Attempt it carries has its startup receipt), commits `LeadNotified.steered` with the machine's steer decision and delivers the text into the live Turn. A delivered window ends its timer. The stall audit (`../stall.ts`) is computed in the same refresh from the fold and Session records; a due audit shares the Claim window and rides `LeadNotified.audit`, once per stalled state. Nudges run only on a running graph. Operator inputs preserve their authority, broadcasts retain their original recipients, and peer messages retain their target Attempt. Each recipient has an immutable delivery ID, `JSON.stringify([sourceMessageId, sessionId])`. Local delivery commits its graph marker with the Session decision. Remote delivery marks it only after a durable worker acknowledgment. `startConstellationDelivery` consumes W's replaying acknowledged stream; repeated receipts remain idempotent and flush pending remote inputs. Connected observations flush them even if the graph already considers the worker fresh.

`applyWorkerDelivery(packet, validate)` is the structural worker adapter. The caller validates the authenticated owner/worker assignment inside the store decision. The store checks a successful command receipt before invoking this validation or examining the current Session. Busy inputs wait for Session events without recording a rejection receipt. Only a newly committed decision runs the Engine effect. Recovery packets are refused until remote interrupted-Turn proof and a continued-Turn receipt are supplied; an owner does not fabricate remote Session facts.

The recovery Continue, and the first brief of an Attempt on a Session the restart cut (or following one), append the restart note: the background commands and Subagents the restart stopped, which will not report back. Plain-session Continue needs the same note from the Engine (contract in `.dagr/contracts/restart-note.md`). `recoverAttempt(candidate)` belongs in the working-attempt resume hook after its slot and lease have been acquired. It commits one machine Continue with `AttemptRecoveryContinued`, then starts the existing Turn with the recovery instruction. The second interruption, the Lead, standalone Sessions, unresolved approvals and stale workers remain for the user. The synchronous `recoverWorkingAttempts` helper is for runtimes whose resume has completed acquisition; asynchronous runtime composition uses the per-Attempt hook.

A silent successful worker end receives one nudge, identified by `AttemptNudged`. A second silent end receives no further Turn and queues a Stopped notification for the Lead digest. An end with background work out is Waiting on Background Work (`../waiting.ts`, ENG-742) and is neither: the Session's live `backgroundTasks` on a Harness that wakes itself (Claude Code), or a `waiting_on` wait the worker declared in that Turn (`AttemptProgressed.waiting`). The wait runs out two minutes after the background work ends (`backgroundChangedAt`), at the declared time plus those two minutes, or an hour after the Turn; one wake per Constellation (`nextWaitExpiry`) then gives the worker the silent end it skipped. The stall audit skips a held wait too. Codex reports background processes but never wakes on them, so only a declared wait holds for it. Blocked Attempts skip nudging. Acceptance of all block targets or a Lead message resumes the worker with AttemptUnblocked and its local Turn in one commit; pending acceptance is re-derived on startup, without polling. A failure settles only the current local worker; stale facts never settle it. `observeWorkerHost` journals ISO `AttemptStale`/`AttemptFresh` facts by Attempt ID without changing Attempt revision.

Handover commits `LeadHandoverRequested` and returns immediately, including a call from the Lead's own Turn. A scoped job waits for the boundary (or interrupts for Hand over now), asks for one summary, then atomically commits `LeadChanged`, the old Session's archive, a fresh Session in the same checkout, and its header Turn. Cancellation and supersession make obsolete completions inert. The header is reconstructed from the log even when the summary fails. Old Lead tokens are revoked and the old Harness is retired; existing worker Attempts are retained.

`engineDeliveryLayer` uses the Engine's existing supervisor and serialization. Final Daemon composition must also provide the local/remote worker provisioning hooks, Harness/MCP attachments, Client Host observations and W's relay services. Those boundaries are injected; this module creates no alternate Harness runtime.

## Evidence

`delivery.test.ts`, `receipts.test.ts`, `../recovery.test.ts` and `../handover/handover.test.ts` exercise real SQLite commits and Session-machine decisions. Quint scenarios and the real-log replay reader cover request ordering, cancelled and superseded requests, old-Lead delivery, stale facts and recipient receipts. `idle.fixture.ts on|off` seeds 128 idle Constellations in a temporary `POLARIS_HOME`; the on variant starts the actual delivery observer and reports its pending timer count for process-tree sampling.

Remote apply queues by Session and immutable packet ID, keeping other Sessions free while a blocked Turn finishes. The Client runs independent delivery RPCs under the worker Connection scope; reconnect cancels old in-flight work and replays pending packets. Session-scoped event subscriptions wake busy unblocks on TurnEnded without polling.

Local worker input jobs wait for admission independently of the graph observer and
other Sessions. The assignment registers a replaceable admission scope with the
EventStore boundary; delivery pins it through commit and execution. A blocked wait
retains its MCP binding and background reporting reservation while releasing idle
capacity. Local and remote paths reacquire before the existing atomic Turn decision,
then revalidate the current assignment inside commit. Stop cancels a queued resume.
